About Mount Cook

Canterbury, South Island

Aoraki / Mount Cook is New Zealand’s highest mountain and one of the country’s most spectacular alpine destinations. Surrounded by glaciers, turquoise lakes, and dramatic mountain ranges, the area offers some of the most impressive scenery in the South Island. Visitors can enjoy walking tracks, photography, stargazing, and views of the Southern Alps.

Why Visit?

Visitors can explore scenic walking tracks, admire glaciers and mountain peaks, enjoy clear night skies, and experience the dramatic landscapes of Aoraki / Mount Cook National Park. Whether you enjoy hiking, photography, or simply being surrounded by nature, this area offers an unforgettable alpine experience.

About Waitomo Glowworm Caves

Waitomo, North Island

The Waitomo Glowworm Caves are one of New Zealand’s most unique natural attractions. Deep underground, thousands of tiny glowworms create a blue-green glow across the cave ceilings, producing an unforgettable atmosphere. Visitors can explore the limestone caves and experience the famous underground boat ride through the glowing passages.

Why Visit?

Visitors can travel through underground caves, see unusual limestone formations, and enjoy a peaceful boat ride beneath thousands of glowing lights. The combination of natural beauty, darkness, and glowing cave ceilings makes Waitomo a destination unlike anywhere else in New Zealand.

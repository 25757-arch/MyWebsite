About Hobbiton Movie Set

Matamata, North Island

Hobbiton Movie Set is one of New Zealand’s most famous film attractions. Located among the rolling green hills near Matamata, it brings the world of Middle-earth to life with colourful gardens, round hobbit-hole doors, and carefully preserved movie sets. Visitors can join guided tours, learn how the films were made, and experience the magical atmosphere of this unique destination.

Why Visit?

Visitors can walk through the famous Hobbiton set, see the hobbit holes, explore the gardens, and discover behind-the-scenes stories from The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit. Whether you are a movie fan or simply enjoy beautiful countryside, Hobbiton offers a fun and memorable experience.
